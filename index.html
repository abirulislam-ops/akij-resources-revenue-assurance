Assumptions & Method

Everything that underpins the numbers, and what still needs business confirmation.
Assumption to confirm #1 — AEL target. FY26 actual revenue was 3,235 Cr, but the FY27 target is 1,985 Cr and is labelled "+55.8% growth". These are inconsistent — confirm the FY27 scope (a line may be divested or excluded) before relying on this target. Even on a −29% trend the projection (2,210 Cr) exceeds it.
Assumption to confirm #2 — portfolio total. The sum of the 16 SBU revenue targets is 9,111 Cr, but the RAP deck's headline total is 7,129 Cr. Confirm which SBUs the 7,129 Cr covers before relying on the portfolio total.

Revenue basis

DWH fin.tblAccountingJournalArc, GL 3010001 (Sales Local) + 3010002 (Sales Foreign), credit entries. Freight (3010005) and Agency (3010006) income are excluded — cost pass-through / commission, not marketing-driven sales. Same basis as the Automated ROMI engine.

Marketing Revenue & Profit

Marketing Revenue = CY revenue − PY same-period revenue (like-for-like; Sep uses the same 9 days of Sep-25). Marketing Profit = Marketing Revenue × FY26 gross margin ((Revenue − COGS 4810001) / Revenue). No ROMI — this is a business-health view, not a media-efficiency view.

Forecast method (seasonal × trend)

  • Each remaining month = prior-year same-month × (1 + Q1-to-date YoY growth), preserving seasonality while carrying the current trend forward.
  • A YoY trend is only used when the prior-year Q1 is a genuine base (≥40% of current Q1); otherwise (new/ramp/pre-launch SBUs) the forecast is held flat at the recent Jul+Aug run-rate.
  • Trend is capped at ±100% to prevent explosive extrapolation.
  • Three scenarios: Repeat FY26 (0% growth) / Current trend / Plan (target).

Data caveats

  • Sep-26 is 9 days MTD; prior-year comparators are like-for-like.
  • AASL (Air) GL revenue is volatile (refunds) and its 190 Cr target conflicts with the 20.16 Cr budget.
  • ACL (OASIS) has a negative FY26 gross margin and is winding down — its Marketing Profit is not meaningful.
  • Mediquip / AgriLife / Landmark are pre-launch (no material revenue yet).
  • DWH GL actuals can exceed the SPA budget-vs-actual "net sales" because GL is gross billing.
